The current list’s own items, as pop_own_box sees them: in a paragraph only what the paragraph added. What \lastpenalty, \lastkern and \lastskip read (tex.web §424, the tail of the current list) must be what \unpenalty, \unkern and \unskip can remove: reading a vertical penalty from before the paragraph that \unpenalty could not remove looped diagram.sty’s \ifnum\lastpenalty…\unpenalty (2605.02221, 2605.25087). The readers skip is_list_tail_transparent items.
