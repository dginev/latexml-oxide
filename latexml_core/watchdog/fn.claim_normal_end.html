pub fn claim_normal_end()
Expand description

Claims the process’s end for the normal path before a single-run binary hands on its result: a breach after it stands down, where its pre-exit hook would overwrite the written result with its placeholder (cortex_worker --standalone --output). When a breach claimed first, parks (park_if_exiting) until its exit.
