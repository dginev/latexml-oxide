1//! jurabib.sty — law-style citations: the raw package, its citation commands handed to the kernel.
2//!
3//! jurabib's `\@citex[annotator][postnote]{keys}` (jurabib.sty:5699) typesets each citation from `\b@<key>`, the
4//! record jurabib.bst writes to the `.aux` (`\bibcite{key}{{Author}{Short title}{…}}`), which LaTeXML never has: every
5//! citation printed pdflatex's first pass — `?, .` in a footnote, the postnote dropped — and registered nothing, so the
6//! bibliography selected no entry and stayed empty (jbtest, the package's manual; Perl has no binding and alike). The
7//! raw package keeps its options, formatting macros and switches; its citation commands become the kernel's
8//! `\@@cite`/`\@@bibref`, which the bibliography stage resolves, as abntex2cite's (abntex2cite_sty.rs) and natbib's.
9//!
10//! The arguments follow jurabib's (`\jb@@cite`, :3760-3762; `\@citex`, :5719-5752): one optional is the postnote; two
11//! are annotator and postnote, swapped under `jurabiborder` (`\ifjb@old@order`) when the first is not empty. The
12//! annotator follows the author after `\jbhowsepannotatorlast` (`/`, :1818) — or, with `annotatorfirst`, precedes it
13//! before `\jbhowsepannotatorfirst` — and the postnote follows `\jbprformat` (:1262) after a space (a comma under
14//! `commabeforerest`). A footnote citation ends with `\unskip.` (`\@cite`, :5704; `\jb@footnote@periodtrue`, :3905,
15//! cleared only by the ibidem/opcit forms BibTeX decides). `\footcite`, `\fullcite`, `\footfullcite`, `\citetitle`,
16//! `\footcitetitle` are jurabib's `\jb…` commands, `\let` at `\begin{document}` (:5949-5955), so the overlay is made on
17//! those; `\footcite*` is `\jbfootcitenotitle` (:3881). `\cite` is a footnote under `super` (`\@citex`'s choice,
18//! :5702-5711; `jb@foot` is the `\jbfoot…` commands' own switch). The citation shows the authors (`Authors`), a full
19//! citation the entry (`FullEntry`), a title citation the title (`Title`).
20//!
21//! jurabib's `\bibstyle` (:1195-1212) replaces the kernel's constructor, which records the style on the bibliography;
22//! both run. jurabib's styles are author-year (each `.bst`'s SORT: jurabib, jox, jureco sorted; jurunsrt unsorted;
23//! latex_constructs `lookup_bibstyle_params`) and leave titles as written (no `change.case$` on titles).
24//! Residuals: the short title jurabib.bst adds when one author has several works (decided by BibTeX, not known at
25//! digestion); several authors joined "A and B" where jurabib writes "A/B" (`\jbbtasep`); the natbib-style commands
26//! (`\citet`, `\citep`, …; no witness); under `super`, a `\cite` inside a document's `\footnote` is a nested note
27//! (jurabib's `\ifjb@fn` is set by its `\@footnotetext` wrap, :3005-3010, which LaTeXML's `\footnote` never runs).
28//! KNOWN_PERL_ERRORS #452. Witness jbtest. Guard `06_cluster_bibliography::jurabib_citations_are_bibrefs`.
