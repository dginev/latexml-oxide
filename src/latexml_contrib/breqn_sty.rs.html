6// breqn.sty (TeX Live 2025, v0.98l): automatic line breaking of displayed equations. The binding keeps breqn's
7// display environments as LaTeXML equations and equation groups and does not read the package raw: breqn.sty
8// typesets each display by measuring and re-breaking boxes (:441-1700), and the flexisym.sty it loads replaces the
9// math characters' mathcodes by macros (flexisym.sty:178-400), so a raw load would leave the formulas without their
10// symbols' meaning. The line breaks are the renderer's to make. Witnesses (68b, binding gaps G3): 0903.2087,
11// 1008.1366, 1107.4667, 1111.2451, 1201.6197, 1302.6047, 1303.0187, 1305.0701, 1306.0788 (`{dgroup}`/`{dgroup*}`
12// undefined), 1606.07330 (`{dsuspend}`), 1201.6197, 1607.06055, 1706.02708, 1811.11912 (`{dseries}`), 2008.13194
13// (`\eqinterlinepenalty`, `\setkeys{breqn}`), 1805.03313 (`\eqlinespacing`, `\eqlineskip`, `\intereqskip`).
