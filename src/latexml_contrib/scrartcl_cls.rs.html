1//! scrartcl.cls — the KOMA-Script article class, raw-interpreted through the
2//! engine (memoir precedent, `memoir_cls.rs`).
3//!
4//! The former stub (`LoadClass!("OmniBus")` + ~30 no-ops, git history
5//! 3c9baade57^) hid the real class: `\DeclareSectionCommand`-defined
6//! headings, `\RedeclareSectionCommand`, `\usekomafont` (which must EXPAND
7//! to the font switches the author put into `\setkomafont`/`\newkomafont`),
8//! `\KOMAoptions`, `\deftocheading`, tocbasic's `\DeclareTOCStyleEntry`
9//! family, `\Ifstr`/`\Ifthispageodd`, `\labeling`, `\dictum`, `\addmargin`,
10//! `\captionabove`, `\captionof`, `\Ifpdfoutput` … — every one an
11//! `undefined:` error or a silently swallowed argument under the stub, and
12//! `\newkomafont{x}{…}` followed by a real `\usekomafont{x}` (loaded later
13//! by a raw scrkbase) died with "font element x not defined" because the
14//! stub's `\newkomafont` never registered anything (witness
15//! contract-example-de/en). The real class raw-loads cleanly under the
16//! engine and yields the correct `<section>`/`<subsection>` structure. Perl
17//! LaTeXML ships no KOMA bindings and does not raw-load the class: it finds no
18//! `scrartcl.cls.ltxml` and falls back to OmniBus, which loads article.
19//! Keeping this binding (rather than deleting the file) makes scrartcl
20//! raw-load under BOTH `[rawclasses]` and the default arXiv configuration.
21//!
22//! Witnesses (perfect-kernel corpus): tikzlings-doc, tikzpingus-doc,
23//! shadethm-doc, fnpara-doc, glossaries-user, LaTeX_RefSheet, easybook,
24//! tutodoc, neoschool, contract-example-de/en, DEMO-TUDa*, tudaexercise
25//! (`\DeclareNewSectionCommand{task}`), bohr/bohr_en (`\recalctypearea`),
26//! arXiv 1802.07175 (`\ifpdf` via scrbase → iftex), 2305.01582
27//! (`\titlehead`), 1702.04336 (scrartcl + tocloft `\sectfont`).
28use latexml_package::prelude::*;
29
30LoadDefinitions!({
31  crate::koma_script::koma_pre_load()?;
32  InputDefinitions!("scrartcl", noltxml => true, extension => Some(Cow::Borrowed("cls")));
33  crate::koma_script::koma_post_load(crate::koma_script::KomaClass::Article)?;
34});
