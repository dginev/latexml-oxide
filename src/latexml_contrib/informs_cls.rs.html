2//! (and informs4a, informs4post, informs4Preprint) and informs5.
3//!
4//! The informs* classes are used by Operations Research, Management Science and the other INFORMS journals. A paper
5//! ships its own copy, which falls back here by version (`informs4`) or by prefix (`informs3noheader`). The class's
6//! front-matter API (\TITLE, \ARTICLEAUTHORS, \AUTHOR, \AFF, \ABSTRACT, \KEYWORDS, …), its float commands and its
7//! theorem sets are mapped onto the frontmatter API and the kernel's floats.
