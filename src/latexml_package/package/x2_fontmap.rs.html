1//! X2 font encoding: the extended Cyrillic of x2enc.def (the LH fonts), which has no Latin letters.
2//!
3//! The upper half and the accents, quotes and dashes are T2A's (x2enc.def declares the same slots: `\CYRGUP` 128 …
4//! `\cyrya` 255, accents 0-10, quotes 16-17, dashes 21-22). The Latin letters' slots hold Cyrillic letters
5//! (x2enc.def's `\DeclareTextSymbol{\CYRAE}{X2}{65}` … `{\cyrizh}{X2}{122}`), as do 13 (`\CYRpalochka`) and 28-31
6//! (`\CYRDELTA`, `\CYRNLHK`); 14-15 are the Cyrillic angle quotes; 25-27 hold nothing (x2.enc `.notdef`). Each slot
7//! decodes to the Unicode letter its command names (x2enc.dfu), Ԛ/Ԝ/Ԩ/Ҏ where the .dfu has none, `\CYRDELTA` as T2B's
8//! δ. Perl has no X2 map: the encoding fell back to OT1, so `\cyrje` declared nothing and printed nothing, and a Latin
9//! letter typed in X2 text printed itself where pdflatex prints the Cyrillic letter of its slot.
10//! OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #777.
