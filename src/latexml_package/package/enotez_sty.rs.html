1//! enotez.sty — raw load; the notes become LaTeXML endnotes and `\printendnotes` lists them.
2//!
3//! enotez gives the list its notes only through the `.aux` round trip: `\endnote` writes
4//! `\enotez@note{id}{mark}{split}{…}{text}` to `\@auxout` (enotez.sty:323-327) and only the next
5//! run's `\enotez@note` (:330-340) fills the property lists `\printendnotes` loops over (:435-471).
6//! LaTeXML converts in one pass with no `.aux`, so the list printed empty and every mark's
7//! `\hyperlink{enz.N}` (:197-212) dangled (skeldoc manual: 15 of 345 words of its notes kept, 15
8//! schema errors; mla-example). Perl has no binding and loses them alike.
9//!
10//! The package is loaded raw — classes build on its templates and internals (mla.cls:285-290,
11//! skeldoc.sty:831 and :860-867, wiley-article.cls:695-700) — and its note internals are replaced:
12//! * the note is recorded in the store `\enotez@note` fills, directly (no `.aux`; its text is not
13//!   kept, since the list is the notes themselves);
14//! * `\endnote`, `\endnotemark`, `\endnotetext` are `\lx@note`/`\lx@notemark`/`\lx@notetext` of
15//!   type `endnote`, as endnotes.sty's binding; an optional mark is the note's mark without a step
16//!   (enotez.sty:236-238); the inline tag follows `mark-cs`/`mark-format`;
17//! * each note is in the list `ent` and in the list of its partition `ent<k>` — the notes made
18//!   after the k-th plain `\printendnotes` (enotez_en.tex:167-170), or a `split` unit;
19//! * `split=section|chapter` advances at the first note of a new unit, found by comparing what the
20//!   unit's headings changed: the counter and printed number (an appendix's section A after section
21//!   1 is a new unit) and the starred-heading counter LaTeXML steps (`\c@UNsection`/`\c@UNchapter`).
22//!   enotez prepends to `\section`/`\chapter` (:884-905), starred forms included, which LaTeXML
23//!   keeps locked. Differences from pdflatex: under `split=section` a `\chapter` heading starts a
24//!   split (pdflatex: only a `\section` after it); an `\endnotetext` whose `\endnotemark` came before
25//!   a heading is listed in the split of the last note before it, titled by the text's unit, so a
26//!   later note under that heading opens another split with the same title (pdflatex: one split, the
27//!   text numbered 0 under `reset`); a `\c@chapter` created
28//!   mid-document (a `\chapter*` alias in a class without chapters) splits once;
29//! * `\printendnotes` prints, through the package's own heading, list name and split titles, one
30//!   `ltx:TOC` per partition (`\printendnotes*`: all notes).
31//!
32//! Presentational keys (`list-style`, the list template's `format`/`number`/`notes-sep`, `backref`)
33//! have no effect: the TOC replaces the typeset list. Dropped with the replaced internals: the
34//! undocumented `\enotezdisable` (:240), an optional mark's `\@currentlabel` (:237; a `\label` after
35//! it names the note by the kernel's tag), and the non-split list's `\prop_gremove` (:634; the
36//! partitions keep the lists apart). The marks' and the list's `enz.N` hyperlinks are not made, so
37//! none dangles; a class's `\enotez_write_mark:nn` (skeldoc.sty:831) is not reached. Guards
38//! `perfect_kernel_batch58::{enotez_notes_are_listed, enotez_split_lists_each_section,
39//! enotez_split_by_chapter, enotez_marks_texts_and_repeated_lists, enotez_split_by_section_in_a_book}`.
