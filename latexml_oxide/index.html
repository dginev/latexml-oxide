install_panic_hook 🔒
Under panic = "abort" (the maxperf distribution build) a panic ends the process without unwinding, so no guard removes the paths the run registered (watchdog::remove_on_exit: the post handoff beside the pages, the page-spill directory) and no guard stops a render worker: the panic hook does, as the exits that skip destructors do, and writes the run’s log (write_panic_log). split_spill_watermark 🔒
The spill watermark of a split run: at most 512 MiB of growth, never above the --max-memory default (a third of the fuse). A split run’s post streams its pages one subtree at a time, so the core is the only whole-document peak left. Nasser’s 2,400-problem book (9,713 pages), serial render: peak RSS 2.01 GB at 1 GiB, 1.35 GB at 512 MiB, 1.39 GB at 256 MiB, user time 128 / 125 / 119 s, pages byte-identical; the default 2 GiB mark of an 8 GiB budget let the eager run reach 3.8-4.9 GB. A document that never grows past the mark converts eagerly, exactly as before. LATEXML_SPILL_AT_MIB overrides it.
