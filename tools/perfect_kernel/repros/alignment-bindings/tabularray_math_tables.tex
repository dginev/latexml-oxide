% widest row: tabularray reads the body whole (`+b`) and tolerates a row wider than the colspec; a math
% alignment is never column-pruned, so the width comes from the body, not a fixed margin (16 empty

% around the math cells. The body read ahead ends at the first `\end{…}` that closes no `\begin{…}` of the
