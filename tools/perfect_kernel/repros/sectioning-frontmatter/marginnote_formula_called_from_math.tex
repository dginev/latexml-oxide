\usepackage{amsmath,marginnote}
\newcommand{\pullbackChiByF}[2]{\pullbackSymbol{#2}#1}
\newcommand{\pullbackSymbol}[1]{#1^{*}}

For a character $\chi$ of $H$ and a morphism ${f \colon G \to H}$, we denote by $\defineNotation{\pullbackChiByF{\chi}{f}}$ the pull-back of $\chi$ by $f$. The pull-back is a character of $G$, with $\pullbackChiByF{\chi}{f}(g) = \chi(f(g))$.
