pdf_creation_date
pdfTeX manual §8.11 \pdfcreationdate (and LuaTeX’s \pdffeedback creationdate): the PDF date string D:YYYYMMDDhhmmssZ of the job start, on the clock \year/\month/\day/\time keep (SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH honored). tex.web §464 str_toks: a conversion’s characters are catcode 12, so the D: is not letters. pdfx.sty-style parsers delimit with a catcode-12 D: (novel-pdfx.sty:298 \gdef\pdfx@getYear D:#1#2#3#4 under \catcode\D=12`); letter tokens missed it and the date parse ran away (novel, class census 2026-09-24).
