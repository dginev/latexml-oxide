A binding declares a construct that states its width as its argument arg (declare_panel_width) and sets its content in a parbox of that width — a {minipage}, a \parbox, a sub-figure — whose \linewidth, \hsize, \textwidth and \columnwidth are that width (latex.ltx:16312-16313 \@iiiminipage; for a \parbox, Perl’s latex_constructs.pool.ltxml:4746 \hsize=#4\textwidth\hsize\columnwidth\hsize, where latex.ltx:16249-16253 \@iiiparbox sets only \hsize and, by \@parboxrestore, \linewidth: the widths are evaluated as we digest the box): its content’s stated widths are evaluated against it (note_stated_widths_in). An \hbox’s (\makebox[w], \resizebox{w}{!}) content is set at the enclosing line.
