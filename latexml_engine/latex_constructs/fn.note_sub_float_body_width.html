A sub-float panel’s stated width is its body’s, never its caption’s: subfigure.sty’s \@subfloat measures the body (subfigure.sty:232-233 \setbox\@tempboxa\hbox{#4}\@tempdima=\wd\@tempboxa) and sets the sub-caption in a \parbox of that width (\@makesubfigurecaption), where our caption is a paragraph of the column’s \hsize (\lx@@caption PBoxContents). Measured with its caption, a captioned panel took a row of its own in arrange_panels: \subfigure[]{…} panels stacked one per row (2006.08949 Figure 2, one row in the PDF; cond-mat9707225, astro-ph0205132, 2012.09475, 1110.0084, hep-ex0004031, 1703.00699). The panel, captioned or not (1007.2355, OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #885), is the width of the body’s material less its \lx@@caption/\@@toccaption, its \lx@labels (a \label makes no box, a \write, where the \lx@label whatsit was sized by its key’s text, as Perl’s, Whatsit.pm:253-265 (0 wide since 79c, KNOWN_PERL_ERRORS #1144; left out here all the same): 0907.1044’s \subfigure[…]{\label{figure:mfexampleofc2} \includegraphics…} was 96pt wider than its graphic) and its spaces, with the natural width of the glue it states (\hskip, \hspace, \quad; an \hfill’s is 0; kerns are kept): the material and glue the source states, not how TeX sets the spaces round it (FIDELITY_CHARTER §2 “Float panels”; the panel grid of SYNC_STATUS’s grid-rule row counts the stated glue between panels the same way; 1307.3388 Fig 3, guard perfect_kernel_batch78x::a_sub_float_bodys_stated_glue_counts). A material of no width is measured again with its glue (1408.2843 Fig 1, a graphic whose file we did not find); only one 0 wide with its glue too leaves the panel its box’s width (panel_width). Perl measured the whole box (latex_constructs.pool.ltxml:3286; OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #876). Witnesses 2511.21020 Figs 7-8, 1607.02761, 2211.12798, 1601.02116 (a label between spaces). Guard perfect_kernel_batch78t::subfigure_panel_is_as_wide_as_its_body.
