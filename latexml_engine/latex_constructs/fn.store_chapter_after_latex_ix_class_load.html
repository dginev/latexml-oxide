OXIDIZED_DESIGN #179, the LaTeX 2.09 \documentstyle document (tex_job.rs): its class keeps the kernel’s \chapter (only a \documentclass retracts it, \chapter:settles), so nothing is put back or cleared, but the “has chapters” answer is stored where the class’s load ends — at its end-of-class hook, after latex209.def’s \@documentclasshook and before \compat@loadpackages loads the style options — and \chapter:stored makes state::class_defines_chapter read it from then on. A refusal the document’s own \def\chapter meets later (\chapter:redefined) is no class’s chapters: \documentstyle{article} + \newcounter{chapter} + \def\chapter#1{…} letters its appendix “A”, as pdflatex, and \documentstyle[appendix]{report} still numbers its appendix chapters “Appendix A”. begin: the refusals recorded before the class load are no class’s either.
