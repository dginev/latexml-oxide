The row tag (EQUATIONROW_TAGS) marking an IEEEeqnarray row whose counters are its number, no pre-advance: \IEEEyesnumber and \IEEEyessubnumber set them (IEEEtrantools’ \global\@IEEEeqnumpreadvfalse, :1402, :1422), and a row given its number by EQUATIONROW_CARRY. Its retraction (a \nonumber or \IEEEnonumber after them, \global\@eqnswfalse, the last one wins) leaves the counters where they are and carries the number to the next row: \IEEEyessubnumber\nonumber \\ c &=& d, pdflatex “(1c)” on the second row; \IEEEyessubnumber\IEEEnonumber after “(4)”, pdflatex “(4a)” on the row after, where this port printed “(4)” again. Witness 2012.02697 (lcmpc.tex:123-136, eq:fpar/eq:par 14a/14b as the .aux).
