The title block of a class whose \maketitle marks its notes in \@fnsymbol — article, report and book (and extarticle, extreport, extbook, copies of them; article.cls:203-205, report.cls:211, book.cls:217, in the \if@titlepage false branch: titlepage, report’s and book’s default (:50-51) and an option of all three, keeps \thefootnote, titlepage_default Some(..), the fallback for the binding’s \if@titlepage after its options), siamltex (siamltex.cls:156-159, no title-page form, None) — when that class is the document’s (is_document_class): the notes of the title, the authors and the date are marked so, and the footnote counter is set back to 0 after \maketitle (article.cls:190, :223; siamltex.cls:175), so the body’s notes count from 1. The marks are made where the frontmatter digests the fields (enter_title_footnotes): \footnotemark[1] in an \author prints “∗” as the text \footnotetext[1] gives after \renewcommand\thefootnote{\fnsymbol{footnote}} (2412.20504, 2006.14582; siamltex 0903.0014; KNOWN_PERL_ERRORS #1060). Perl’s classes mark them as the body (\thefootnote). The class’s \thanks (and \footnote, which its \maketitle lets to \thanks) is \footnotemark and its text, so each steps the footnote counter, under a title page too (article.cls:173, :241, report.cls:179, :247, book.cls:185, :253; latex.ltx:17215 \thanks): the block’s frontmatter notes step it as the \maketitle digests them (lx_title_counts_footnotes, \lx@title@footnote@step), and \footnotemark[\arabic{footnote}] after an author’s \footnote is that note’s “∗” (2201.11183 “Zhenan Fan∗1, Huang Fang∗2”; 2111.13260 “Suhani Vora1 *, Noha Radwan1 *”).
