Adds to a reference’s properties the text a label of another document prints (record_external_label), as pdflatex prints the \r@<label> xr defined: reftext, refclass (ltx_ref_external, after ltx_nolink for a starred reference) and refhref. Post drops the text and the class where this document holds the label after all, as LaTeX’s own .aux, read at \begin{document}, redefines the \r@<label> xr made in the preamble.
