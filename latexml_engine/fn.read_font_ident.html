tex.web §577 scan_font_ident: <font> = <fontdef token> | \font | <font range><4-bit number>, after the next non-blank non-call token. The identifier is read WITH expansion, so \fontdimen8 \ifx#1\displaystyle \textfont\else…\fi 3 selects \textfont and the conditional completes. Perl reads it unexpanded (TeX_Fonts.pool.ltxml FontDef), which took \ifx as the font and orphaned its \else/\fi (arXiv 2605.21425: a \mathpalette underline macro, 1000 “not in a conditional”, Fatal).
