An amsmath display starts ({gather}, {align}, {multline}, {aligned} … and their starred forms): the labels its own \label makes (mark_display_label) name the step held here, not one made in a row. amsmath’s \label there is \label@in@display (amsmath.sty:1230-1236), which only stores the key; \make@display@tag makes the label in the row’s tag cell (:1240-1252, :1621-1627 {gather}, every row), after the cell group a \refstepcounter was made in restored \@currentlabel — \begin{gather*} u=v \refstepcounter{foo}\label{e6} \end{gather*} is the section’s 7 in pdflatex. names_the_step: the display’s labels may name the step current here ({gather}, and an {aligned}/{split} in an {equation*}: pdflatex 44, 45). Not for the align family ({align}, {flalign}, {alignat}) and {multline}, starred or not: \math@cr@@@align makes the display tag only under \iftag@ (:1833-1843), so an untagged row’s label is the next tagged row’s (\df@label carried) or none (pdflatex ??), never a step held at the start (repro cross-references/amsmath_row_label_names_the_display_step.tex, review probe rev78g2 al2.tex: \begin{align} a&=b \label{n2}\notag \\ c&=d \end{align} in a \refstepcounter environment is equation 1). The kernel’s \label a document saved before (\let\originallabel\label at \begin{document}, the \labelrel idiom of 2310.19138) labels at once, after the cell’s step (“a”). Local to the display; an inner one ({aligned} in {gather*}) keeps the outer’s. The outermost start forgets a display label announced and never made (mark_display_label).
