The raw step (note_raw_refstep) a \label made now names: \@currentlabel is still the step’s \the<counter>, not text a package defined since (\protected@edef\@currentlabel{…}); for an amsmath display’s own \label (mark_display_label) the step current where the display started (hold_raw_refstep_for_display).
