Perl defmath_wrapped (Package.pm:1786-1813): no arguments, but a presentation naming control sequences. \cs expands to \cs@wrapper{\cs@presentation}, the presentation macro holds the tokens as given, and the wrapper constructor puts their digested form in an XMWrap carrying the DefMath’s attributes (name, meaning, role, scriptpos, stretchy, …), which the math parser moves onto the parsed content (MathParser.pm:333-360, parser.rs). It reverts as its argument, or as \cs in a dual’s content branch.
