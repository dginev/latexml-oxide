The property a math alignment’s row carries when it prints a number that must survive though its cells are empty: latex.ltx’s eqnarray number on a row of its own under an unnumbered row (x &=& y \cr \end{eqnarray}, the \@@eqncr of the end), which rearrange_eqnarray joins to the equation above. Normalization keeps such a row; it is stripped with the other _ attributes at finalization.
