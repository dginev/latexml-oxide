pub fn spill_mark_kb() -> Option<u64>
Expand description

The resident set (KB) at which this conversion spills: spill_watermark_bytes above its starting RSS, never closer to the fuse (process RSS, it guards the machine) than two watermarks — the headroom the fuse fraction was calibrated for (a half-of-fuse mark crept into the fuse, spill_watermark_bytes). With the default third-of-fuse watermark that cap is the watermark itself, so under a fuse the mark is absolute, as before the conversion’s baseline (revSTRM: a worker near its recycle threshold must not spill later than the calibration allows); the baseline moves the mark only without a fuse or under a smaller calibration watermark. Order independence rests on a switched conversion writing the eager XML, not on the mark. A zero watermark (a test’s or a calibration’s LATEXML_SPILL_AT_MIB=0) puts every seam over it.
