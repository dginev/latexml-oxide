Expand description

One family’s association conventions, as its binding declares them (declare_frontmatter_conventions).

Fields§

§name: &'static str

the declaration’s name (reported with every decision it made)

§binding: &'static str

the declaring binding’s file name, as the BINDINGS registry gives it (<name>.<extension>), and the hook (\lx@add@authors@append) it applies to: * any

§hook: &'static str§inst_after_comma: bool

an \inst mark (the convention’s own, the rehearsal’s \lx@fm@obs@mark) after a comma is the name’s before the comma, glued or not (llncs.cls:894-896 and aa.cls:759-760 \def\inst#1{\unskip$^{#1}$}; OD #807, 2401.16957); any other mark after a comma is the name’s before only when glued to the comma

§and_inline: bool

\and is inline text joining the names of one row, not a column (llncs.cls:1013-1018 \def\and{\unskip,…} in \@maketitle; OD #792: Ann Able \and Bob Baker\\ University of Somewhere is both names’)

§author_lines: bool

the \\ lines of the class’s \author paragraph are read as the binding’s own split read them before the block went to the reading whole (llncs’s \lx@llncs@author until Z18e, OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #984: its reads_as_affiliation per line): under a row’s affiliation line, a line reading as no institution over one reading as one starts a new row of names, and a line whose name carries an ORCID is names. Off for the kernel, whose \\ lines keep their affiliation reading (an article’s “Einstein Drive” over an address line, hep-th0212274)

§break_parts_names: bool

every \\ line of the class’s \author paragraph is names, as its title page sets them (webofc.cls:497-552: \@author a paragraph whose lines are names, the institutes apart: the template’s \\The SND Collaboration a creator, 1609.01040), the binding’s spelling split at \\ until P2-1 (OD #993). Off for the kernel, whose \\ lines under names keep their affiliation reading

§label_sequence: bool

a labelseq=<role> entry is labelled by its place among the entries of that role (llncs’s and aa’s \institute{A \and B}, numbered 1, 2 as the class prints them: OD #791, #159)

§email_owner: EmailOwner§call_is_group: bool

each call is an author group of its own (an e-mail nothing else places is the group’s only author’s: iopart’s \author, OD #808)

§title_marks: TitleMarks§thenote: bool

authblk’s notes (authblk.sty:114-118, 148-151): an unlabelled \author shows \AB@thenote, the affil counter stepped at the first \author after an \affil, and an unlabelled \affil is labelled by it; the marks show when a label is given or the counter passes \c@Maxaffil (state_counter), else the affiliations print under the run of authors (block mode)

§state_counter: Option<&'static str>

a counter the policy reads at each block’s call (the ledger’s counter)

§and_word: bool

\and inside an annotation prints the word “and” (amsart.cls:599 \def\and{\unskip{ }\@@and{ }\ignorespaces}): the entry’s text holds it

§unmarked_notes: bool

the title’s notes print no mark (IEEEtran.cls:4756 \def\thefootnote{} in \maketitle, “the \thanks{} mark type is empty”): a note stands beside a name only by its place, so its inner contacts are a name’s only by spelling (main-session decision 2 of 2026-10-22, user ruling 2026-10-20 #4)

§email_in_place: bool

the class’s \email prints its address where it is called (llncs.cls:897 \def\email#1{{\tt#1}}, aa.cls:824 e-mail: {\aa@emailfont #1}, svjour3.cls:303, svmult.cls:2304, svjour.cls:256): one inside a document’s note (\offprints{O. Kochukhov, \email{…}}, a \thanks) prints in the note, after the name the note gives, which designates its owner — while the \email meaning is one of the class’s bindings’ (ledger::email_meaning, read at the call; Resolver::answer_document_notes). Not amsart’s (amsart.cls:538 \def\email stores the address for the author’s address block) nor REVTeX’s (the author’s footnote): there the address is the call’s current author’s, as the binding routes it

§keyed_labels: bool

a call’s label= is a key, never printed: the class prints the entry’s place in its list instead (elsarticle.cls:656-662 \theaffn, elsart.cls v2.18 :1135-1164 \theaddress), so no footnote’s mark answers it, a symbol’s neither (FIDELITY_CHARTER §4 R1: an author whose keys request affiliations takes none by another route; 1108.0201’s \address[*]{current address} under M.~Pesce-Rollins\footnotemark[1]) — an author’s own keys (annotations= of \author[a,b]) and a mark typed by hand do, the places keys in order spell (0907.5014’s Denes Molnar$^{a,b}$)

§block_breaks: &'static [(&'static str, BreakKind)]

the commands that part a block’s lines or columns whatever they print here, as the class’s title page does, each declared by the binding owning the \author they stand in: OmniBus’s no-op \alignauthor a column (inst_support_sty.rs; what sig-alternate.cls, sigchi.cls print, whose \alignauthor read raw is the tabular it makes), ijcai’s \affiliations and \emails a section (ijcai19.sty:124-130). A rehearsal notes their calls beside the block’s events (Outcome::Events breaks), which they do not part; no reading reads them yet

§title_page: TitlePageDecl

what the class’s title page prints that the stand-in for article.cls’s page (sect05.rs title_page_print_tokens) cannot know: TitlePageDecl

§cite: &'static str

what the declaration reproduces
