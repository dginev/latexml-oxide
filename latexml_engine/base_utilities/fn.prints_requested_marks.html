pub fn prints_requested_marks(arg: &Tokens, caller: &str) -> Result<bool>
Expand description

Whether arg prints a mark of its own (argument_marks) whose label a queued name requests (requested_labels); unread, its tokens’ marks, counted for caller. A class’s numbered store declines its numbers for its own marks only when the names ask for them (revP4d S1: \ma printing \textsuperscript{a} under \inst{1}, numbered 1 and 2).
