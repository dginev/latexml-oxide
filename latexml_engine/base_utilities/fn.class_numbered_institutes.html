A class-numbered institute list as its title page prints it: llncs.cls:870-885 \institutename (copied by preprint.cls:233-256, 2405.16615) redefines \and to step the institute counter and print $^{\theinst}$ before the next entry, the first numbered 1 — so the n-th \and entry of the \@institute store is affiliation n, which the authors’ numeric marks pick ($^{1}$, $^2$). stuff with each entry led by its number as a superscript, when the class numbers the entries of its store (named by its control sequence, \@institute), the store holds at least two \and entries and no mark of its own, both read by meaning (the kernel’s \and a macro holds, a mark a macro prints: split_at_kernel_separators; a mark a name requests, prints_requested_marks), and the queued authors’ marks request numbers (numbered_marks_requested); else None (the entries annotate every author, \lx@add@store@affiliations). Whether the class numbers them is what its own title page printed in the speculative run frontmatter_stores.rs note_and_numbering made of the store (lx_and_numbered_<store>: the sentinel entries each printed after its number, in \and order — sab.cls:404-426’s and edpsci.cls’s \refstepcounter{inst}, 2308.05831, 2308.05830, 2501.08874, baaa.cls 2206.02830, comsis2.cls 2207.09249; batch 104b); with no such run (no store, none could be made), the class’s \institutename read as before: a \def\and stepping a counter (\stepcounter). Was each author given every institute (2405.16615; SYNC_STATUS row 153; the sab family, SYNC_STATUS row “104b”).
