Run a compiled binding’s loader (name.extension, the binding’s file: revtex4_support.sty), noting it as the owner of each tracked command’s meaning (OWNED_COMMANDS) when its load changed it and no binding it loaded did, or its own code redefined it after one did (revtex4-2.cls’s binding loads revtex4_support.sty, whose \author it is; aa.cls’s loads aa_support.sty, whose \email it is, and defines its own \author after inst_support.sty’s): the binding half of the policy key (author_binding), and an e-mail call’s binding (email_meaning).
