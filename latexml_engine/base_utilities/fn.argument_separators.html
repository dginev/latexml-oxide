pub fn argument_separators(arg: &Tokens) -> Result<Option<Vec<ArgSeparator>>>
Expand description

The \and-family separators arg holds at its own level (nest 0: not one a layout container of it holds, a tabular, a \parbox, nor a note’s), in order, each with what it means where the query is made (SepMeaning). None where no rehearsal could be made — a call inside one (no journal opens), over the queries’ allowance, an argument past the field’s positions, a run whose assignments could not be undone (Rehearsal::Skipped) — or where the run left a group open (a \begingroup with no \endgroup: no kernel author line holds one, so its separators are unknown, not a list). What a rehearsal leaves for the reading, each put back (by reading rehearse‘s writes to its thread-locals and its global assignments): the reads it spends and the calls it leaves out (READS_LEFT, LEFT_OUT: the queries’ own allowance instead, rehearsal::for_a_query), the footnote steps it carries for the next block’s rehearsal (lx_fm_obs_counter_seen, lx_fm_obs_steps_carried, restored here), its flags and its assignments (restored and rolled back by rehearse; a run whose assignments could not be undone keeps them, as every rehearsal’s does, SkipReason::NotUndone, and answers None).
