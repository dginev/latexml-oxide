A marker-less line that is purely a list of email addresses — a shared \texttt{a@x, b@y,} / \email{...} / \{a,b\}@x line covering all authors. Like line_is_email but tolerates a comma-separated list (and the trailing comma authors often leave), as email_addresses reads it: every item a full address or a braced list of local parts before its domain. A prose affiliation line (“Dept. of Foo, University of Pisa, Italy”) is rejected, so this never misclassifies an address as an email.
