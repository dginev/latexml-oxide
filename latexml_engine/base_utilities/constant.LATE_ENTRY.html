A frontmatter element set after \maketitle placed the frontmatter (frontmatter_push): queued after \lx@frontmatterhere’s flush or digested in place after it. A late abstract’s cover is followed by the first late abstract and its run, save what was printed before the cover (printed_before_the_cover: smfart’s {abstract} before \maketitle and {altabstract} after it). Internal, stripped from the output.
