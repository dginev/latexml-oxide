Perl augmentDelimiterProperties’ stretchy (TeX_Math.pool.ltxml:810-811) for a delimiter outside DELIMITER_MAP (stmaryrd’s \llbracket, a \delimiter glyph): the element the digested delimiter became is marked stretchy. Until 65d such a delimiter warned “Missing delimiter; ‘.’ inserted” and stayed unstretched (RED math-parse/left_delimiter_outside_the_map); it was accepted by meaning when it was scanned (scan_delimiter).
