pub fn settle_cover_title_ink(document: &mut Document)
Expand description

At \end{document}, the cover-title marks still in the tree (OXIDIZED_DESIGN #629), by the XSLT’s own test (LaTeXML-structure-xhtml.xsl:374-375): while the document has a top-level ltx:titlepage, its <ltx:title> is not displayed and the class’s print is the title the page shows — kept, its marks removed, wherever the cover’s typesetting put it (a \vbox print the absorber set beside an emptied cover: hep-th0010274); without one (a titlepage nested in a section or a box, every cover unwound or demoted) the title is displayed and the print goes (drop_cover_title_ink).
