pub fn first_ink(d: &Digested) -> Option<String>
Expand description

The first item of digested material not known to print nothing (leaves_no_ink), described: its command, or its text. Known to print nothing are

  • a box of white space (an interword space, a tie’s no-break space) and a box Perl marks isSpace (glue, a kern, \quad, a phantom, \indent/\noindent: Box.pm:33-38 and the pool’s isSpace properties), but a leader;
  • a rule with no width or no height and depth (a strut);
  • a line or page break, \par, a penalty, \leavevmode (INKLESS_COMMANDS);
  • a transparent wrapper whose arguments (but options and a position keyword) and body print nothing: a plain box or a formula (INKLESS_WRAPPERS: \makebox[1em]{}, \raisebox{1ex}{}, $ $), or a constructor whose whole construction is a template printing only its arguments or body (prints_only_arguments: \mbox{}, a font switch, $\mathbb{}$).

Anything else is ink: a constructor’s own text, with or without a font (revsymb’s \openone, "1" in the blackboard font; \dots, \LaTeX, $\vdots$), an element of its own (\textsuperscript{}’s ltx:sup, a framed box \fbox{}), a closure-built construction (\hypertarget{a}{}) or one with a construction hook (\emph{}), an empty template standing for what it does not show (the observer rehearsal’s \lx@fm@obs@url, its footnote mark), a leader (\hrulefill), an image, a cross-reference, an alignment, an undefined command’s error marker — and any item, blank or not, whose font carries a background colour: \colorbox{c}{…} is \hbox{\pagecolor{c}…} (color.sty, color_sty.rs), whose \pagecolor leaves an empty box in the coloured font, and pdflatex prints the coloured block (\colorbox{red}{}, \colorbox{yellow}{\hspace{2em}}, inside \mbox{} too).
