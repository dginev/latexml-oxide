The combining characters a math accent whatsit prints on its base, from the glyph its constructor carries (accent, set for an OVERACCENT or UNDERACCENT operator, latexml_core transfer_common_constructor_options): \bar’s U+00AF over is U+0304, \hat’s ^ U+0302, \vec’s → U+20D7, a combining glyph itself (\mathring’s U+030A), \underline’s U+00AF under U+0332; None for anything else, an accent whose glyph has no combining form (\overbrace’s ⏞) among them. The frontmatter label readers (queue_digest::label_ink, the observer’s events::script_ink) write it after the base: $^{\bar{\dag}}$ is the label †̄, no \dag’s †, and $^{\hat a}$ â, no a (review rev92b_r4 S2; \={x} digests to x̄ already).
