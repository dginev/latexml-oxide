Whether the lines under an unmarked author line’s names read as more names and nothing tells them from an institution: each a name by its words, styled as the names line is, with no address, link, note or mark on it (Ann Able\\ Bob Baker, Ann Able\quad Bob Baker\\ Cat Cole; 2610.38214’s \\ Stefano Soatto under two rows of names; not Ann Able\\ Meta AI, an acronym beside a word in small letters, as flag_merged_creators reads a line, nor \\ \textit{Univ X}, nor \\ University of X). The kernel’s read gives such a line to the names above it as their affiliation (Perl’s first-line rule), a name made an affiliation where it is one; a binding that reads an \author as one name otherwise must not route it there unread: authblk’s \author (2610.38214). A row of names parted by horizontal space goes on with the names first (space_parted_rows_joined), as the kernel reads it.
