pub fn reset_field_provenance()

Reset every piece of a conversion’s field-provenance thread state (OXIDIZED_DESIGN #715): the stamped gate, the field ledger and its settled flag, the pending cover. Run at the start of every conversion (the converter’s reset_conversion_state, at initialize_report), whatever the thread converted before: a persistent cortex_worker thread, a streaming restart and the CLI’s supplements convert on one thread without an engine reset, and a ledger settled by the previous document recorded nothing for the next one, which then lifted no print at all (review r4 finding 1).
