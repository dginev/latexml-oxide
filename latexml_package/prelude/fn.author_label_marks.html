The marks an author’s name shows that read as labels (reads_as_labels), outside its notes and not inside a word (rewrite_in_word_superscripts: Craig M$^{\mathrm{c}}$Rae spells the name): what the name requests, kept as it prints. For authblk’s \author kept whole on \lx@add@creator because text follows its marks (Bob Baker$^{1}$\textsuperscript{equal contrib.}, Bob Baker$^{1}$ (bob@x.org)), which still requests the affiliation its mark picks, as a marked name read as a list does — it requested nothing, and lost $^1$Univ X to the author before it (77n r5 review probes k1, k2, k5). The binding’s contacts (authblk’s \affil inside \author) are left out with their arguments: their marks label them. Only the labels are requested (label_pieces): a footnote symbol ($^{*}$, $^{1,\dagger}$’s dagger, the \ast glued to 1\ast) is the name’s own text, which a request nothing answered showed a second time after it (Bob Baker$^{*}$ (bob@x.org) printed “Bob Baker∗ (bob@x.org)∗”; 2605.10201’s $^{1\ast}$, $^{1\dagger}$ gained “∗,†” in its name; 77n r6 review probes s1, s3, s5, z1).
