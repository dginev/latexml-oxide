The text digested material typesets, as ink_text reads it, with where its words end: a space for a whatsit that prints nothing (a line break, \par, a skip), and INK_SEAM at the edges of a whatsit that prints (a font switch, a box, an accent) and of each alignment cell, where the text runs on in print but a line may end — an accent built as an alignment runs on (combelow’s \cb{s}, Babe\cb{s}-Bolyai “Babes”, 2007.07526), an author table’s cells end their words. The title page’s printed words (base_utilities::frontmatter::title_page_check).
