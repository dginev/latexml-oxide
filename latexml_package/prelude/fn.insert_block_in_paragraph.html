insert_block for a box that is paragraph material: its definition’s \leavevmode (enter_horizontal) left TeX in a paragraph, the whatsit’s in_paragraph property (Constructor::digest_to_whatsit). latex.ltx’s \parbox ends in \@iiiparbox and {minipage} in \@iiiminipage, which begin with \leavevmode (16249-16250, 16305-16306): between paragraphs the box starts one, and the text after it continues that paragraph (KNOWN_PERL_ERRORS #309, DIVERGENCES #338).
