Where a line’s unprinted lead ends: its spacing (leading_spacing_end) and its declarations (opening_declarations), as often as they interleave (\vspace*{2mm}\noindent, \small\vspace{1mm}). Public for authblk’s \affil (latexml_package), which reads whether a line opens with a mark past it.
