Whether an author argument holds a separator the kernel’s author read parts it at: as that read sees it (normalize_hspace_separators: \hspace, \hfill and a spacing run between two names as \quad; the space a name sets before its own note or mark none) it splits at author_group_splits or at the line breaks of author_affil_splits into two pieces or more that are not empty. split_tokens leaves a note’s argument, a group and math alone. For a binding that reads an \author with none as one name: authblk’s.
