Whether digested material typesets something a reader sees: a character that is not a space, an image (a whatsit carrying a graphic or image candidates), a cross-reference or citation, whose text the post-processor fills in (fills_generated_text), or a constructor whose template prints text of its own (template_prints_text, \LaTeX), anywhere in it — through lists, the whatsit arguments it shows (shown_arguments, as ink_text reads them) and bodies, and alignment cells. Paragraph and spacing commands, rules and empty boxes are not content, nor is an argument LaTeX only tests: the position letter of \begin{tabular}[t], \parbox[t], {minipage}[t], \makebox[w][l] or \shortstack[l] (a Position parameter, base_parameter_types.rs) — counted, the deproc title code’s \begin{tabular}[t]{l}\bf\rule{\z@}{24pt}… with an emptied \@author (iclr*_conference.sty:83-93, arxiv.sty, colm, corl, nips) kept its \@maketitle deposit, an empty strut table before the first section (HF14; 94 of 6,000 sampled papers, 2610.20409, 2310.10873). A whatsit’s own string is its reversion (Whatsit.pm toString), so a digested \noindent\par stringifies as source; this walks the boxes instead. Used to keep a speculative typesetting (a class’s \maketitle replay, a one-shot page overlay) only when it shows something.
