Pair a note’s mark and text: Perl’s relocateFootnote (latex_constructs.pool.ltxml:498-510) matches their marks exactly; here spaces are ignored, so a mark printed as set (\tablenotemark{c, d}, deluxetable_sty.rs shown_mark) pairs with the text given c,d. A counter’s mark has no space, so a \footnotemark’s pairing is Perl’s. A mark and a text that print no common mark, one of them in the title block — or neither printing a mark (title_block_partner) —, pair by the footnote counter value they stand for (_fnvalue, footnote_pairing): article’s title block marks \footnotemark[2] “†” where a \footnotetext[2] after \maketitle prints “2” (2505.14146; pdflatex prints the two apart, the author pairs them), a class whose title marks are not known (siamltex: 1406.0089, 1503.07490), and \fnsymbol’s “∥” against \@fnsymbol’s “‖” for 6 (2002.11721). Either way a mark and a text on the two sides of a class’s counter reset after \maketitle pair only as same_count admits. KNOWN_PERL_ERRORS #1060, OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #851.
