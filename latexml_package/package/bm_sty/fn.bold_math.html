Expand description

The expansion of a bold-math command cs (\bm, \boldsymbol) whose bounded bold constructor is bold (\lx@bm@bold, Perl’s \bm, bm.sty.ltxml:20, and \boldsymbol, amsbsy.sty.ltxml:23, under their own names). bm.sty’s \bm (\bm@general, bm.sty:162-217) scans its argument token by token and emits the bold result at the level \bm is used at, grouping only the argument’s own {…} groups (\bm@gr@up): \left and \right are kept as they are and the delimiter after them becomes a bold \delimiter (\bm@delimiter, :416). Here the argument is bolded by the bounded bold, but a \left, \middle or \right at the argument’s top level is taken out of it: since 65d \left opens a math_left_group (OXIDIZED_DESIGN #530) that an argument group would close before its \right (2204.13665, \bm{\left\langle} #1 \bm{\right\rangle}: “\bm Attempt to close a group that switched to mode math”, 587 errors). The command and its delimiter stand between the bolded runs, followed by \lx@bm@delimiter, which makes the delimiter bold. Only an argument whose delimiters do not pair up is split so: a pair inside it stays in the bounded bold, whose group holds the math_left_group whole, so the fenced whole keeps its scripts and primes (\bm{\left(A+B\right)^\mathsf{T}}, \bm{x \left( y \right)_\text{L}}). For a BoldArgument::Grouped command the split comes with a Warning: TeX’s box ends the \left there. OXIDIZED_DESIGN #544.
