u_fontmap
The U (“unknown”) font encoding: glyphs come from the FAMILY. A raw \DeclareSymbolFont{AMSa}{U}{msa}{m}{n} (oz.sty:34, amsfonts users) then decodes through the per-family map U_msa_fontmap (font_decode; AMSb’s msb through U_msb_fontmap, which amsfonts’ \newsymbol reaches), which Perl (no u.fontmap.ltxml) leaves undecoded. The Euler Roman family eur (amsfonts’ ueur.fd, eurm/eurb) decodes through the math italic layout, OML, at the 111 slots it shares with cmmi: its TFMs say “TEX MATH ITALIC SUBSET”, tftopl gives no slot outside cmmi10’s, and rendered they draw cmmi’s glyphs upright (program.sty:834-835’s \powersetsymbol ℘ at “7D, older sn-jnl.cls copies; statex, beilstein.cls, mismath, mathgreeks, sansmath raw); the 17 slots it lacks, 37 and 38 (ϱ and ς, “Euler’s missing variants”, sansmath.sty:76), 40-47 (harpoons, hooks, triangles), 63 (star), 91-95 (flat, natural, sharp, smile, frown) and 126 (the vector accent), stay undecodable (U_eur_fontmap), and are recorded as gaps of the font (U_eur_fontmap_lacks): a kernel name declared there — \star at “3F, \vec at “7E — is U+FFFD with the font’s Warning, as pdflatex prints nothing there (OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #27). So are msbm’s five gaps, “5F and “62-“65 (U_msb_fontmap_lacks: amssymb’s AMSa \varpropto, \Subset, \Supset, \Cup, \Cap slots, which a name declared in AMSb would otherwise decode by name); msam has none. Every family’s gaps are generated by tftopl over each size its .fd loads (U_FONT_GAPS, tools/perfect_kernel/gen_reference_math_slots.py --u-font-gaps); the maps’ other empty slots are glyphs with no single character (msbm’s negated pairs, msam’s \dabar@), no gaps. The encoding has no map of its own, bbold loaded or not (its blackboard map is its own family’s, U_blackboard and U_bbold, bbold_sty.rs; OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #914): a U symbol font of another family (shuffle.sty, stmaryrd’s stmry) stays undecoded — U+FFFD, and one Warning per symbol font (OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #27) — but for a kernel or AMS name declared at its fontmath.ltx or amssymb slot (sect08.rs reference_symbol, OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #910) — and the text switch \fontencoding{U} falls back to OT1 while \f@encoding still names U (\lx@fontencoding, tex_fonts.rs; OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #645). Guards: perfect_kernel_batch54::declare_math_delimiter_defines_the_symbol, perfect_kernel_batch68v::u_symbol_font_without_a_map_stays_undecoded, perfect_kernel_batch89c::euler_roman_decodes_as_math_italic. Witnesses: 2306.04672 (\shuffle); 2205.13841, 2212.02709 (program.sty’s U/eur via sn-jnl.cls copies; neither prints \powerset, so no census paper shows the ℘).
