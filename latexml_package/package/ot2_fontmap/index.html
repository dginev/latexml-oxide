Module ot2_fontmap

OT2 font encoding: the Washington University Cyrillic fonts (wncyr, ot2enc.def), Cyrillic in the Latin slots.

Each slot decodes to the glyph wncyr10 draws there (wncyr10.afm’s names: A А, C Ц, Q Ч, V В, W Щ, X Ш, Y Ы, J Ј …), the letters as ot2enc.def names them (\DeclareTextSymbol{\CYRNJE}{OT2}{0} … {\cyrhrdsn}{OT2} {127}, Unicode from ot2enc.dfu): 32, 36, 38 and 64 are the accents of \", \U, \' and \u, 61 the dotless i of \.{\i} (і); the fonts have no upper half. The transliteration’s digraphs are the fonts’ ligatures (sh ш, ch ч, zh ж, shch щ, ya я, e1 э …: ot2_ligature). Perl has no OT2 map: the encoding fell back to OT1, so \CYRZH printed nothing and {\fontencoding{OT2}\selectfont Moskva} printed “Moskva” where pdflatex prints “Москва”.
