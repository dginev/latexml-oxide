hyperref’s \href and its \url (hyperref.sty:4801 \DeclareRobustCommand*{\url}), with the reader and the constructor they share; href/url choose which of the two user commands are defined. The ar5iv preload gives a document that never loaded hyperref the ones it lacks at \begin{document}, as arXiv’s build, which loads hyperref into every paper, does (ar5iv_sty.rs, OXIDIZED_DESIGN #588).
