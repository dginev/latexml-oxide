tone.sty (tipa’s tone letters, FUKUI Rei): the raw file, with its tone letters as Unicode’s.

\tone{51} (tone.sty:13-14) draws a contour from tipa’s tone-letter parts — slots 128-145, each a segment raised to its level (\@outputtone, :37-58), not a character — and the stem (slot 191) at its right; \rtone (:16-17) puts the stem at the left, \stone (:19-20) draws short segments. Unicode writes the contour as its tone letters, one per level (UAX: Chao tone letters, U+02E5-02E9 with the stem at the right, U+A712-A716 at the left), which fonts join into the contour: \tone{51} ˥˩, \rtone{15} ꜖꜒. A level outside 1-5 keeps tone.sty’s construction, whose tone-letter parts have no character, and says so. OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #781. Witnesses 2601.19932, 2602.10003 (\usepackage[tone]{tipa}, \tone{…}).
