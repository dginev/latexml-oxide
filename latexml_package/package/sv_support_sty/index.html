define_bold_vectors
The Springer classes’ bold vectors, the two forms their \vec takes (KNOWN_PERL_ERRORS #1143): \lx@sv@vec@mathbf is \mbox{\boldmath$\mathbf{#1}$} (svjour3.cls:631-636, svjour.cls:546-551 under \if@mathematic; svmult.cls:1015-1030 and svproc.cls:389-404 by default), upright bold letters and bold italic Greek (“v + ω”, CMBX10 and CMMIB10 in pdflatex); \lx@sv@vec@boldmath is \mbox{\boldmath$#1$} (llncs.cls:393-397 under \if@custvec, the svjour classes without \if@mathematic, svmult and svproc under vecphys), bold italic (CMMIB10). Each reverts as \vec; the class wraps the first in \ensuremath where it does. Their \mathchoice of four \mboxes, one per style, is the bold font of the current style here. Witnesses 2512.18788, hep-ex0701025 (svmult), 2609.05466 (svjour3).
