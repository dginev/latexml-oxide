Expand description

A binding declares the title marks of its stand-in copy’s \maketitle, read after it (content::read_stand_in_copy: jmlr2e, neurips, ijcai), or of one the copy’s \AtBeginDocument code defines: at \begin{document}, by the body TeX records (read_copy_title_marks). The lines wait in the state, under the copy’s file, with the number its stand-in copy gets.
