cjs-rcs-article.cls (The Canadian Journal of Statistics, TeX Live 2025): the class is loaded raw, and the title-page environments it makes with environ.sty’s \NewEnviron — {englishabstract}, {frenchabstract} (:379-382 \global\let\CJS@englishabstract\BODY) and {keywords} (:383-386) — keep their body in stores only its \maketitle reads (:414-480: each abstract under \selectlanguage{<language>}\paragraph{\abstractname}, the keywords as an inline list joined by “; “, :387-393), which the kernel’s locked \maketitle never runs: both abstracts and the keywords were lost (2608.13762). The environments keep the class’s environ form, their body handed to the frontmatter instead of the store: each abstract an ltx:abstract in its language, named as the class names it, the keywords with their items joined as the class’s list joins them, ending in its “.” (:387-393 itemjoin={{; }}, after={.}). {classification} (:394-404, [\CJS@defclassifname] = “MSC2020” by default, its \CJS@classiflist joined and ended the same way, :401-404) is the same shape: the frontmatter’s classification named by \CJS@classifname, as the class’s \maketitle heads it (:474-478).
