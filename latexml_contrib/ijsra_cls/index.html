ijsra.cls: the class is loaded raw, with its frontmatter slots free and its abstract environment semantic.

The class keeps its frontmatter in \def slots the document fills: \def\abstract{…}, which \IJSRAopening prints through \begin{IJSRAabstract}\abstract\end{IJSRAabstract} when \ifdef\abstract (ijsra.cls:475-479), after \newcommand\abstract{}\undef\abstract{} (:372-373) left it undefined. Under the kernel’s lock on \abstract (sect05.rs, as Perl, pool:1136) the document’s value was refused, so the bare use opened a frontmatter capture inside the \list of {IJSRAabstract}, whose \endlist landed in it: the text was lost (Error \endlx@list). This class’s name is free of that lock (as amsppt’s, amsppt_sty.rs), so \ifdef\abstract means what it says, and an ijsra document without an abstract prints none. {IJSRAabstract} (:415-420), the opening’s and a document’s own second one (ijsra.tex:410, \foreignlanguage{french}), is the kernel’s {abstract}, its keywords line (:417) a ltx:keywords. The \list, \footnotesize\sffamily and \itshape are its print layout. Witness ijsra/ijsra (TeX Live manual); guard perfect_kernel_batchm1::the_ijsra_abstract_is_semantic. Open: \IJSRAopening prints the \authorone… slots as a {center}, so the document has no ltx:creator.
