pub fn parse_forest_keylist(tokens: &[Token]) -> Vec<ForestKey>

Splits a forest keylist the way pgfkeys does (forest hands a node’s specification to pgfkeys as content=<spec>, forest.sty:1423-1426 new node, and processes it at :6050 process keylist=given options): items at unbraced commas (pgfkeys.code.tex:357 \pgfkeys@@normal#1,), the key at the first unbraced = (:369 \pgfkeys@unpack#1=#2=#3), each part trimmed (pgfkeys_trim); an empty key is skipped (:372-373).
