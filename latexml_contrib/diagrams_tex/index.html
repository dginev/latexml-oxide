Paul Taylor’s diagrams package (diagrams.tex v3.97, 2024-11-20; www.PaulTaylor.EU/diagrams, CTAN macros/generic/diagrams/taylor). Not in TeX Live (its licence forbids alteration), but in arXiv’s TeX tree, so ~470 arXiv papers build with it (\diagramstyle 467, \newarrow 441, \rTo 362 papers undefined in run 349). Perl’s ar5iv-bindings diagrams.tex.ltxml discards every {diagram} as <ltx:ERROR>{diagram}</ltx:ERROR>; this binding keeps the diagram, after the model of amscd’s {CD} (amscd_sty.rs): a math array of objects and labelled arrows (OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #589). Witnesses: math0001049, math-ph0001006, math0003019, math-ph0007001, math0010298, math0012073, math0102115, math0104065, math0104175, math0109209, math0310311, math0404431, cs0204036; plain TeX \input diagrams: math0105166; the amstex option: math0104206, math0403203.

The macro surface is the manual’s (manual.tex v3.97, “Typing the diagram”, “Labels”, “Parallel arrows”, “Defining arrow styles”, “Diagonal lines”, “Options”), checked against the use in the 100 witness papers:

  • \begin{diagram}[options] … \end{diagram} (plain \diagram … \enddiagram): cells &, rows \\ (the package’s \cr, with no [dim]). In maths it is a matrix of the formula; outside maths it is displayed (“it is better to remove such enclosings”, manual FAQ “Display options”), but in a table cell or an LR box, as with [inline], it stays in the text line; [LaTeXeqno] numbers it with LaTeX’s equation counter (“You can put a \label inside”). The options are read only when [ follows at once (manual “Options”: after \begin{diagram} and a new line a first cell may begin with a bracket).
  • An arrow \<dir><Name> for the directions r l u d ru rd lu ld, followed by [options], (x,y) (the cells a diagonal spans), positional {f}{g} (very old versions) and the labels ^ above, _ below, < left, > right, ~ middle, each a single token or a braced group. “For reverse compatibility, above=left and below=right for vertical arrows”; for negative gradient diagonals (rd, lu) above=right and below=left. Outside a diagram the source reads no span and no </> (\switch@arg, diagrams.tex:301-306). Horizontal arrows “may also be used in text, $A \rTo^f B$”; one outside maths is the package’s error, as is a vertical arrow outside a diagram (in maths or not); a diagonal one outside a diagram raises nothing.
  • \newarrow{Name}{tail}{filler}{middle}{filler}{head} defines the eight arrows of Name (diagrams.tex:449, the definitions made at the caller’s group level with \aftergroup; a tip or middle equal to its filler is empty; ignored within maths), and the arrows the source declares in its section (24) are predefined, with its synonyms (\rInto, \hLine, \NW, …).
  • \pile{\rTo^f \\ \rTo_g}: parallel horizontal arrows, a one-column array.
  • Layout only, no output: \diagramstyle[…] (but its amstex loads amscd’s {CD}), \newarrowhead/tail/middle/filler, \newdiagramgrid, \HonV, \VonH, \HmeetV. The pullback corners \SEpbk etc. are a visible mark not emitted (open, OD #589; math0203179, math0306018).
