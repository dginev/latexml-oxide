epl2.cls (and its predecessor epl.cls, whose general and bibliography macros it took over: epl2.cls:1328, :1344): the EPL (Europhysics Letters) class of the Italian Physical Society, not in TeX Live and seldom shipped, so the papers fell to OmniBus and stopped at the class’s bibliography macros, \Name first (740 run-336 papers: 0911.3858, 1904.03227, 1602.05654). Read from epl2.cls v1.1 (2006-2012, as shipped in arXiv 1201.0004), which is written from scratch after article.cls (:13-16); here the article class with the frontmatter routed to the frontmatter API. Rust-only: Perl has no EPL binding. A paper shipping the class gets this binding too, as a binding outranks a raw class.
