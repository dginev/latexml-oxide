bmcart.cls (BioMed Central’s article class): the papers ship the class (it is not in TeX Live), and it is read raw (OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #444). Its {frontmatter} lets \title, \author and \address to its printers (:966-984 \set@frontmatter@cmd): \title@fmt typesets the title in the {fmbox} (:1034-1053), \author@fmt the name with the marks of its addressref keys (:1198-1255), and \address@fmt stores the address by its id (:1287-1296) for the “Author details” block that \thebibliography prints (:1708-1712 \printaddresses), which the kernel’s bibliography constructor never runs. Read raw, the document had no title, no creator and no address (1711.01177: the title page as boxes, the marks “??”). So, after the read, the three printers are the frontmatter’s, as elsart_support_core’s \author[labels]/\address[label] are (elsart_support_core.sty.ltxml:35-42): each \author a creator requesting its addressref/presentaddressref ids, with its email addresses; each \address an affiliation labelled by its id (the class’s aff<n> when it has none); and the {fmbox} the box only holds them, its “Full list of author information …” line (:1002 \auto@set@thanksbox) a print flourish. Perl has no binding: OmniBus, whose \author read “[” as the name. Guard perfect_kernel_batch78m::bmcart_frontmatter_is_frontmatter.
