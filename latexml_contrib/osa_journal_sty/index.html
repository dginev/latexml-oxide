Module osa_journal_sty 

Source
Expand description

The Optical Society of America’s submission styles for the article class: opex3.sty (Optics Express, v1.0 2003), ol2.sty (Optics Letters, v1.0 2006), osajnl.sty (the OSA journals, v2.0 2004) and its successor osajnl2.sty (v2.1 2006), loaded as \documentclass{article}\usepackage{opex3}. Not in TeX Live and often not shipped, so the papers stopped at \address (759 run-336 papers: 1006.2188, 0901.2165, 0901.2997, 0901.2793); a shipped copy, read raw, typeset the title, authors and addresses into the text, leaving no title or authors. They share one document syntax (cited from the copies in 0901.2165 (opex3.sty:43-67), 0901.2997 (ol2.sty:38-89), 0707.0735 (osajnl.sty:51-90), 0901.2793 (osajnl2.sty:47-86)): \title, \author (the names, marked $^1$ by affiliation), \address/\affil (the affiliations, marked alike), \email, \homepage, \ocis (\pacs), an {abstract}, the reference list as {thebibliography} or {References}, and the journal abbreviations of the references; one binding serves them, routing the frontmatter to the frontmatter API. Rust-only: Perl has no OSA binding.
