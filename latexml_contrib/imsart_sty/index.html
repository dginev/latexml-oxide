The IMS imsart.sty support file, as a binding of its own: the frontmatter and keyword markup the imsart.cls binding owns over it (crate::imsart_cls::imsart_sty_markup, \arxiv), for a paper that loads imsart.sty without that class. A shipped copy is read after it as the stand-in’s copy (OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #731), its own extras added: imsart.sty under its own name beside \documentclass{article} (1301.1466), and a renamed copy that declares itself \ProvidesFile{imsart.sty} (the declaration route, content.rs provided_binding) — 1905.11425’s and 2101.10506’s stylefile.sty (loaded by their stylefile.cls, a renamed imsart.cls), math0608476’s imsart_mod.sty. Read raw, \ead[label=e1,mark]{…} inside \author glued “label=e1” into the name (OmniBus’s \ead{}[]) or lost the e-mail; now each is its author’s contact (OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #986).
