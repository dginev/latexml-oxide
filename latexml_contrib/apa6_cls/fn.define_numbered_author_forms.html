pub fn define_numbered_author_forms(mask: Option<&str>) -> Result<()>
Expand description

The numbered author and affiliation forms of apa.cls and apa6.cls (apa.cls:427-459 is apa6.cls:745-777 line for line), handed to the frontmatter at \maketitle, where the class reads them: mask names the class’s author-masking flag (apa6.cls:126 the mask option, read at :1143 and :1378; apa.cls has none, None).

The title page picks its layout by which numbered names are defined (:1145-1214, :1381-1414: \@authorTwo … \@authorSix, each checked only when the one before it is). A name slot may hold a group of names over its one affiliation (1401.2054 \twoauthors{Zhiyong Zhang, Kaifeng Jiang, Haiyan Liu}{In-Sue Oh}, 1402.6720 {Edgar C.\ Merkle and Dongjun You}): the slot is split as the kernel’s \author splits its names, appended to the slots before it (\lx@add@authors@list), and its affiliation given to each of them (annotate=new: the creators of this slot, the earlier ones having theirs). A numbered form also sets \@author and \@affil to its first name and affiliation (:747, :762), so once \@authorTwo is defined the numbered names and affiliations are the title page’s whatever \author and \affiliation said: the creators the kernel’s \author made are cleared (2011.06172 \author{Author} “hack around some bugs in apa.cls” beside \threeauthors), and so is the one-author affiliation store, which K11 would otherwise give every author at \maketitle (harvest_stores; 1811.01821 \affiliation{University of Cambridge} beside \twoaffiliations). Without numbered names the title page sets \@author over \@affil, a numbered affiliation form’s first one included (1605.09288, 1609.04156 \author{…} with \twoaffiliations; the class warns “More affiliations than authors defined”, :785): K11’s own.
