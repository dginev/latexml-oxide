jinstpub.sty — JINST’s SISSA/IOP publication style (2015/08/05 v.1.0 and 2020/09/18 v.1.1638, shipped with the papers, not in TeX Live): jheppub’s frontmatter — the accumulating \author[affil]{name}, \affiliation[label]{…}, \emailAdd{…} and \keywords/\abstract stores its own \maketitle lays out (jinstpub.sty:60-150, 176-252) — with JINST’s extras, as jcappub_sty gives JCAP’s. Read raw, the affiliations were an itemize, the e-mail a paragraph and the author’s \note{Corresponding author.} text in the personname (2006.05543, 2303.10224; 23 of the 30,000-paper sample). The older JINST class (JINST.cls, JHEP3-derived) has its own binding, latexml_contrib jinst_cls.rs. Neither Perl nor Rust had a jinstpub binding (so it is a contrib binding).
