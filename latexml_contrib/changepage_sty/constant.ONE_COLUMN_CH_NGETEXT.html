\ch@ngetext in LaTeXML’s one column. changepage’s (changepage.sty:80-88), chngpage’s (chngpage.sty:79-88) and memoir’s (memoir.cls:11237-11245), which \changetext/\changepage end with, halve \columnwidth under \if@twocolumn; LaTeXML sets one column, so \columnwidth is the changed \textwidth and \linewidth follows it (every later width=\linewidth graphic at half width otherwise).
