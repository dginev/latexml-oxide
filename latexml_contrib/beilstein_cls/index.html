beilstein.cls (Beilstein Journal of Nanotechnology / Organic Chemistry): the class is loaded raw, and its author block — \author (beilstein.cls:586-697: \author{name}, \author[marks]{name}, \author[marks][e-mail]{name} (:627 \@@@author, the e-mail listed with the others, :649-651), the corresponding author’s \author*[marks]{name}{e-mail}) and \affiliation{…} (:704-737), which number the affiliations in order and give each to the author just named, building accumulators (\authors, \affiliations, \emails) that only its \maketitle reads (:739-823) — becomes the frontmatter: each author a creator carrying its marks and the numbers of the affiliations that follow it, the corresponding author’s e-mail a contact, each affiliation a contact labelled by its number. Under the raw class (the rawclasses lever) the kernel’s locked \author refused the class’s, so a starred author became an author named * with its name and e-mail as stray affiliations, and every affiliation was lost (2203.09951, 2604.06890). An author is handed on when the next one starts or at \maketitle, once the affiliations that follow it are known.
