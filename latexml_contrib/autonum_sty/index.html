autonum.sty binding (no Perl binding). OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #895.

autonum numbers only the equations a reference names: its \ref/\eqref/\cref record the labels they name in the aux file (autonum.sty:330-345), and on the next run its display environments, renewed at \begin{document} (:127-140, :186-280), drop the numbers of the rest. A single pass has no earlier run to read. Raw-loaded, its renewals also reach only the environments a binding does not lock: {split}’s ran inside our locked {align}, whose own renewal — the one that restores \label at its end (\autonum@restoreSubcommands) — never did, so the display’s \label was left global and every later \label failed (“\lx@ams@display@outer@label undefined”, the label lost: 1905.00606). The binding loads autonum’s packages and options and leaves the displays to amsmath’s binding: every equation keeps its number, as without autonum.
