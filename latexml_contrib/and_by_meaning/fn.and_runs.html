The \ands of tokens (AndRuns): each token spelled as one of spelled, whatever it means where the binding reads it (the parts the binding cut before P2-5). l_aa, interact and pasj00 print their own \and where they set the block, over a document’s renewed one (l-aa.sty:412 inside \institutename‘s group, interact.cls:266 \def\author#1{\def\and{and }…}, pasj00.cls:1878 \def\and{…and…} in \maketitle). lipics (v2019+: a global \def\and{\newline}, lipics-v2021.cls:758), ecai (ecai.cls:889, global) and gretsi (no \and of its own) honour a renewal, so a written renewed \and should not cut there: today’s spelled cut is kept for them pending the helpers’ unification (Kernel-only, eptcs cells’ rule; revP25 finding 1). And each separator the query reads meaning the kernel’s \and: held in a macro of the document’s, a \let copy (its own run), which the spelling missed.
