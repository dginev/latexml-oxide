The author separators of a binding’s argument, found by meaning as well as by spelling (Option C step P2-5, OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #995): the gates and the cuts of l_aa, ecai, interact, pasj00, gretsi and lipics part at each \and written in the argument, as before, and at each separator the query (argument_separators) reads meaning the kernel’s \and — held in a macro of the document’s, a \let copy —, which the spelling missed. A written \and stays a separator whatever the document made it (trap T8): these classes set their own \and where they print the block (and_runs), and where they do not (ecai, gretsi) today’s cut is kept. P2-4’s eptcs cells (eptcs_cls.rs) and P2-2’s institute lists cut at the kernel meaning alone through the engine’s shared kernel_separator_runs (observer/separators.rs, P22b); and_runs is written apart and moves onto it at the helpers’ unification.

And each separator the query reads meaning the kernel’s \and: held in a macro of the document’s, a \let copy (its own run), which the spelling missed.
