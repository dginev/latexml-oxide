jpconf.cls (Journal of Physics: Conference Series, IOP’s conference class, 2004/05/04 v1.0): the papers ship the class (it is not in TeX Live), and it is read raw (OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #444). Its address and e-mail blocks (:104-110: \eads, \ead, \mailto, \address, each an {indented} list item where it stands) are iopart’s (iopart.cls:240-248, 363-364), so after the read they are the frontmatter iopart_support makes of them (iopart_support_sty::iop_address_and_email): each \address an affiliation of the authors before it, each \ead an e-mail placed with its author, where read raw they were bullet lists after the abstract (0705.0202, 1011.0523). Perl has no binding: OmniBus’s \address and \email. Guard perfect_kernel_batch78m::jpconf_address_and_email_are_frontmatter.
