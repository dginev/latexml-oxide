Module webofc_cls

webofc.cls (EDP Sciences’ Web of Conferences: EPJ, MATEC, E3S, ITM, SHS and BIO Web of Conferences): the papers ship the class (it is not in TeX Live) and it is read raw, its packages loaded as it loads them. Its title page is the A&A/Springer \inst form, which the shared inst_support binding gives aa, llncs and the Springer classes: an \author list split at \and, \inst{n} naming the n-th of the \institute list (webofc.cls:434-438, where \inst typesets $^{#1}$ and the institutes are an \and list the title page numbers; read raw, the name kept the superscripts and every author got every institute: 2301.07438 “Andrea Shindler,1”, 1411.7792, 2610.23498). The e-mail is its author’s contact (webofc.cls:440 prints “e-mail: “ and the address, inside a \thanks or not; a \thanks holding only the e-mail leaves no note, the kernel’s \lx@annotate@frontmatter@now: 2409.01693), and \fnsep, the comma between two footnote marks (:217, aa’s \fnmsep), sets nothing before a \thanks in the frontmatter (the kernel’s digest_frontmatter_item). \sidecaption (:824-857) sets the float’s body beside its caption, which is layout: the caption stays the float’s <caption>, as \sidecaption in aa_support and sv_support (read raw it typeset “Figure 1. …” as text in a \parbox; the html_feedback report on 2610.23498). Its \@makecaption (:788-812) prints the label and its period bold, “Figure 1. …”, the figure’s and table’s title format here (caption_sty::class_makecaption_label), unless the document loads caption, which replaces it. A paper whose source lacks the class falls to OmniBus, with the loader’s missing-file Warning.
