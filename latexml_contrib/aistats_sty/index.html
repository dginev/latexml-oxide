aistats.sty — the AISTATS conference styles (aistats2012.sty, aistats2014.sty … aistats2027.sty, which the versioned fallback strips to aistats; aistats2026.sty, and the 2010-2011 aistats2e.sty and its margin variant aistats2eM.sty, by their own names): one family binding (as Perl’s icml.sty.ltxml serves the ICML years, icml_support.sty.ltxml:34ff). The paper’s copy is read raw, then its title-block setters, which print in place inside \twocolumn[…] and never reach \title/\author (aistats2027.sty:173/248/355, aistats2eM.sty:102/171/252, the same in every year), are the frontmatter’s:

  • \aistatstitle{T} is the title (\title); a later one prints in place as the copy does (the supplement’s “Supplementary Material” heading, 2610.11580), where a second \title would be dropped;
  • \aistatsauthor{A₁ \And A₂} followed by \aistatsaddress{a₁ \And a₂} are the authors, each column of the address under the author of its column when the row has several columns and the address as many (A₁ \\ a₁ \And A₂ \\ a₂: name, affiliation and e-mail lines, as the kernel \author reads them; 2604.21432, 2605.03921, 2610.11580, 1002.4058) — also across material between them that prints nothing (\runningauthor, \vspace, a definition: 2003.03080, 2402.12508, 1302.5348), which runs first; otherwise the names, and the address shared by them (\lx@add@affiliation@shared): each column an affiliation of the row, its e-mail lines the authors’ (1806.02924’s four names over one Carnegie Mellon address, 2510.09534’s one column of names apart by \quad), or, marked, each line to the authors whose marks it names (1910.09056’s $^1$University of British Columbia, $^2$…, a row of one column). Each \aistatsauthor row adds to the ones before. Their arguments are read where they stand (in_place: 1302.5348’s \UMDAddress).

What the PDF shows decides (user ruling 2026-10-06): a submission under review (\statePaper 0, no accepted or 2027 preprint option) prints “Anonymous Author” and “Anonymous Institution” where the copy says so (aistats2020.sty … aistats2027.sty; 2e, 2012, 2014 and 2019 print the names either way). Rebinding \aistatsauthor also retires the copy’s running-head measurement, whose \PackageError{Document}{Running heading author exceeds size limitations} (aistats2026.sty:289-295) the former aistats2026 stub pre-empted with a placeholder \@runningauthor (47 papers, stages 15-20). Perl has no aistats binding: the setters print in place there too, or are undefined (3 errors). OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #772. Witnesses 1002.4058, 1302.5134, 1302.5348, 1303.0449, 1103.0941 (2e), 1202.1568 (2eM), 1102.1492 (2012), 1311.1894 (2014), 1806.02924 (2019), 2604.21432 (2020), 1910.09056 (2022), 2405.15172 (2025), 2605.03921, 2510.09534 (2026), 2610.11580 (2027); 2101.00218 ships aistats2021.sty unused.
