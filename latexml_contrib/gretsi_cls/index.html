gretsi.cls, the class of the French GRETSI conference (not in TeX Live; papers ship it). Two releases, whose frontmatter commands conflict, so the binding follows the paper’s copy (shipped_class_text), and v1.3 where there is none:

  • v1.2 (2008/12/15; 45 of 62 papers, 1001.1912’s copy): \titre, \auteur (= \author) of \coord{first}{last} {mark} names, \adresse (= \address) of \affil{mark}{lines}, \email, the abstracts \resumefrancais/ \frenchabstract and \resumeanglais/\englishabstract, and \legende for captions.
  • v1.3 (2023/01/25; 2304.10248’s copy): \titre, \auteurs (= \author) of \auteur{first}{last}{email}{mark} names, \affils of \affil{mark}{lines}, and the abstracts \resume (French) and \abstract (English).

Both store the abstracts and print them under the title, the French one first, whatever order the paper gives them in.
