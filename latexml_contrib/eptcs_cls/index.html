eptcs.cls (Electronic Proceedings in Theoretical Computer Science; v1.5 2010/11/26, v1.6 2020/12/20, v1.7 2022/05/20 — the same title page in each): the papers ship the class (it is not in TeX Live) and it is read raw, its packages loaded as it loads them (\LoadClass[letterpaper,11pt,twoside]{article}, hyperref, mathptmx, eptcsdata.tex when the paper has one). Perl has no binding.

Its title page (eptcs.cls:160-191) sets \@author in a centred tabular, so \and starts a column of its own and \institute{…} and \email{…}, written inside \author after the names, are each \\ and a line under them: \author{Ann Able \institute{Univ A} \email{ann@a.edu} \and Bob Baker \institute{Univ B}}. Read as the kernel’s author line, the institute and e-mail stayed in the name (“Ilaria De Crescenzo Università degli Studi di Salerno Dipartimento di Informatica”, 1408.5969; 1909.07676, 2406.16409, 2109.08295’s “Jens Lehmann SDA Research Group, … {grubenmann, jens.lehmann}@cs.uni-bonn.de”). The binding writes the block as the class prints it and gives it whole to the reading (\lx@add@author@split, OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #984): each cell a column — cut where the block holds a separator meaning the kernel’s \and, written, held in a macro or a \let copy (the separator query, #995; P2-4, OD #991 (8)), and followed by that separator’s own tokens, which the reading cuts at — its names (Tobias Grubenmann and Jens Lehmann, 2109.08295) and each \institute and \email a \\ line under them; the reading places each line — an institute every name’s of the column, an e-mail the author it spells, else, a list of one address per name in order, the name at its place (1101.4424’s g.stavrinos@… \qquad tassiana98@…), else the column’s only author, else the block’s (FIDELITY_CHARTER §4). A cell that opens with a tabular of its own — the names as its columns, a row of e-mails under them, and lines under the table (1407.7928) — or with an environment of the paper’s own that opens one (2409.15014’s {authors}{5}, its \institute and \email lines spanning the columns) is read the same way: a column’s names its cell, a full row’s cell a line under its column, any other line under the table every name’s. A block with none of these is the kernel’s.
