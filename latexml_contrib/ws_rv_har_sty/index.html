ws-rv-har.sty — World Scientific’s renamed copy of natbib 7.x (\ProvidesPackage{ws-rv-har}, ws-rv-har.sty:16, “derived from natbib.sty”), the author-year citations of its review-volume class ws-rv9x6 (the document’s \usepackage{ws-rv-har}, 2301.03442; 1602.07982 ships it and loads ws-rv-van). A stand-in for the author’s file (author_files.rs AUTHOR_FILE_STAND_INS): it is natbib, so the natbib binding loads with its options (njdnatbib_sty::natbib_fork_options), and the paper’s copy, read after it, only provides (OXIDIZED_DESIGN_DIVERGENCES #731). Read raw, its \citep found no \bibitem its \bibitem had read: 2301.03442’s 28 citations “(?)”, where pdflatex prints “(Mayor et al. 2011)” (\bibliographystyle{aa}, natbib’s \bibstyle@aa).

The default it changes that the PDF shows: a class with chapters heads the bibliography \section*{\bibname} (:681-685; natbib \chapter*{\bibname}), one without \refname (:677) — by its \@ifundefined{chapter} (:676, state::class_defines_chapter): ws-rv9x6.cls:499 \newcommand\chapter (its \bibname “References”, :91, 2301.03442’s PDF); amsart (:686-687) \refname. Its punctuation defaults (:244-249: (, ), ;, ,) are natbib’s; its superscript postnote in parentheses (:256-259) shows only under super.
