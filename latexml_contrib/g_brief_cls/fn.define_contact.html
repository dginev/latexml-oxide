pub fn define_contact() -> Result<()>
Expand description

\lx@gbrief@contact{role}{label}{store,…} and \lx@gbrief@sender{name store}{store,…}. The contact: the set stores’ values joined by \\, a contact of the current creator with the class’s label (\adresstext, a constant of the class’s language option) as its name; nothing when every store is blank. The sender: a creator of role sender named by the name store, or, without a name, an empty one for the contacts — none when nothing is set.
