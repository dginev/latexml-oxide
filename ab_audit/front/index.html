Expand description

Frontmatter tuples (design §5 Stage 0): per creator its role, name, contacts and notes, plus the document’s title, abstract and keywords; and the comparison of two documents’ tuples.

A creator lost or gained, a contact emptied, a name now empty or an address newly split into runs is what sample statistics missed (63w4: an orcid slot swallowed the next author of 2605.02715; 64k5: 41 empty contacts; 64zc r1: a typewriter address split by a $\sim$, 1601.02576, linked to one character).
