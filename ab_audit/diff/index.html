Expand description

One paper, two conversions (compare), or one conversion (audit): the flagged row.

It fails toward flagging (CLAUDE.md “Canvas signal integrity”): a document missing on either side, an unparsable XML, a log without its Status:conversion:N line, a timeout or an extractor panic is a regression row, never a pass.
